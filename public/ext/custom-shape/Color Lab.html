What is HSL, actually?

Open Source Arras builds almost every color using Hue, Saturation, and Lightness instead of red/green/blue. Drag the wheel and sliders below — watch the swatch change.

hue: 200°
0° and 360° are both red — it's a wheel, not a ruler.
0% = grey, no matter the hue. 100% = fully vivid.
0% = black, 100% = white, 50% = the "purest" version of the hue.
